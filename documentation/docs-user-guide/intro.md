# Welcome to Metadata Capture

Welcome to the Metadata Capture User Guide!

**Metadata Capture** is your tool for managing your dataset catalogue while ensuring compliance with the Data Catalogue Vocabulary Application Profile - Luxembourg ([DCAT-AP-LU](https://mindig_lu.gitlab.io/DCAT-AP-LU/)). 

As your first step towards secure and compliant data sharing, Metadata Capture enables your organisation to document the types of data you hold and make them discoverable to users within and outside your organisation.

**This guide is for data professionals**—data holders, catalogue managers, data stewards, and anyone responsible for cataloguing, reviewing, and sharing datasets in compliance with DCAT-AP-LU standards. Create, publish, and manage datasets throughout their lifecycle—from creation to publication and deprecation.
