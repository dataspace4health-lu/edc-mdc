## Overview

**Submit datasets for review** after you have completed and reviewed all dataset details. By submitting datasets, you initiate the review process, allowing designated reviewers to validate the accuracy and completeness of the metadata before approval and publication.

To understand the full review process, see: [The dataset record lifecycle](/user-guide/key-concepts/the-dataset-record-lifecycle).

## Submit datasets for review

To submit datasets, you must be an Editor or have the permission to **add datasets**. Check your [permissions](/user-guide/check-your-permissions).

1. Go to the **Draft** tab in the datasets panel.

   <img src={useBaseUrl('/manage-datasets/draft-tab.png')} alt="Screenshot of the draft tab" width="550" />


2. Select **View** on the dataset you want to submit. 

3. Review the dataset details and select **Submit Dataset**. The comment field appears. 

4. (Optional) Enter your comments to provide context to reviewers or other collaborators. 

5. Select **Submit Dataset** to confirm submission. After the dataset is submitted, it appears in the **Completed** tab.

    <img src={useBaseUrl('/manage-datasets/submit-dataset.png')} width="750" alt="A screenshot of the submit dataset button" />

:::tip What's next?

Now that you have submitted the dataset, reviewers can now [validate the dataset](/user-guide/validate-a-dataset) from the **Completed** tab.

:::
