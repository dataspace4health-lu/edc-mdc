# Check your permissions

Your ability to perform actions on datasets depend on the [roles and organisations](/user-guide/roles-and-permissions) assigned to you. You can hold multiple roles and be part of multiple organisations. Each role has granular permissions that determine what actions you can perform within that organisation.

:::info Role names may vary

Role names may vary in on your organisation. If you're unsure about your roles or permissions, consult your [Inventory Coordinator](/user-guide/roles-and-permissions) or [System Administrator](/user-guide/roles-and-permissions).

:::

<br/>

To check your roles and permissions:

1. Select the profile icon (<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" style={{display:"inline",verticalAlign:"middle",width:"1em",height:"1em"}} fill="currentColor" viewBox="0 0 20 20"><path fillRule="evenodd" d="M18 10a8 8 0 11-16 0 8 8 0 0116 0zm-6-3a2 2 0 11-4 0 2 2 0 014 0zm-2 4a5 5 0 00-4.546 2.916A5.986 5.986 0 0010 16a5.986 5.986 0 004.546-2.084A5 5 0 0010 11z" clipRule="evenodd"/></svg>). Your current organisations and roles appear below your name. 

   In this example, the user is part of two organisations: *Commerce Bureau* and *National Statistics Office*, and is also a Catalogue Manager in both organisations.

   <img src={useBaseUrl('/get-started/orgs-roles-below-your-name.png')} alt="Profile icon with organisations and roles displayed below name" width="400" />

2. Select the role you want to view. The **Role Directory** opens with the list of roles and permissions.


   :::note The Role Directory

   While the Role Directory shows all roles and permissions, you can only perform actions allowed by your assigned roles.

   :::

   In this example, the user belongs to only one organisation as a Data Manager.

   <img src={useBaseUrl('/get-started/role-directory.png')} alt="Role Directory showing permissions for different roles"  />

3. To request additional roles or organisations, contact your [Inventory Coordinator](/user-guide/roles-and-permissions) or [System Administrator](/user-guide/roles-and-permissions).
